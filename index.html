📌 Facts worth knowing

🛢️
Well depth Conventional wells reach 1,000–5,000 m — similar to oil wells, often the same reservoir.
💧
Fracking threshold If methane leaks exceed ~3% of production, the climate benefit over oil disappears entirely.
🇺🇸
US shale revolution In 2005 the US planned to import LNG. By 2023 it became the world's #1 LNG exporter.
🇷🇺
Russia's collapse Russia supplied 40% of Europe's gas before 2022. By 2024, that fell below 10%.
🌾
Food security ~50% of nitrogen in human bodies comes from fertilizers made with natural gas as feedstock.
🏗️
Infrastructure lock-in Gas pipelines last 40–60 years. Infrastructure built today will still operate past 2080.
